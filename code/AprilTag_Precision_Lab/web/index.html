Forward, left and up here are the base directions you want the arm to use, not left and right in the MuJoCo view. First sample a comfortable start position; then, starting from the same point each time, move the Tag at least 4 cm forward, left and up and sample each. Keep the simulation paused while calibrating; the three directions should be mutually perpendicular.

② Accuracy measurement

For the static test, fix both the phone and the Tag. For the displacement test, hold still for 2 s at the start and end points and measure the true displacement with a ruler. Measure orientation with a fixed-angle jig. The screen shows the raw, unsmoothed minimum-reprojection solution and warns explicitly when the pose is ambiguous.
